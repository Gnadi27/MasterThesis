\chapter{Verification and validation of the numerical framework}
\label{chap:numerical-verification-validation}

% Establish the credibility of the OpenFOAM/HiSA numerics before turbulence
% models and wall functions are introduced. The chapter should distinguish
% code/solution verification from validation and connect each benchmark to the
% numerical or physical capability that it tests.

Verification assesses whether a mathematical model is implemented correctly and
its equations are solved with sufficient numerical accuracy, whereas validation
assesses how accurately the model represents physical reality for its intended
use.\footnote{Terminology follows AIAA G-077-1998 as summarized in the NASA
NPARC Alliance glossary: \url{https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/wind/valid/tutorial/glossary.html}.}
For the OpenFOAM/HiSA framework introduced in Chapter~\ref{chap:numerical-framework},
analytical and numerical reference comparisons and convergence studies provide
verification checks, while comparisons with measurements provide validation
evidence. The assessment covers attached-shock angles and post-shock states
(Section~\ref{sec:numerical-vv:shock-angle-post-shock}), detached-shock stand-off
distances and carbuncle behaviour
(Section~\ref{sec:numerical-vv:shock-stand-off-carbuncle}), laminar velocity and
temperature profiles
(Section~\ref{sec:numerical-vv:laminar-boundary-layer-profiles}), laminar heat-transfer along a flat-plate and through a compression-corner interaction
(Section~\ref{sec:numerical-vv:laminar-heat-transfer-compression-corner}), and
adiabatic turbulent velocity profiles and skin-friction
(Section~\ref{sec:numerical-vv:adiabatic-turbulent-boundary-layer-profiles}).
Experimental comparisons concern detached-shock stand-off distances and the
adiabatic turbulent flat-plate. These checks assess the framework's capabilities
and limitations before the cold-wall turbulence-model and wall-function
evaluation in Chapter~\ref{chap:comparative-evaluation}.
