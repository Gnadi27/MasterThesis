\section{Scope, modelling assumptions, and limitations}
\label{sec:introduction:scope-assumptions-limitations}



The scope limited itself to solely zero-pressure-gradient flat-plates, because already there the TMs poorly performed and important insights on the applicability of wall functions was found. In the spirit of Dr. Wilcox "Quote", it is necessary to continue this work to more complex canonical flow cases such as the FPG, APG, and the different SWBLIs, after it can be shown that the TMs are capable of predicting the flow significantly better for hypersonic cold-wall ZPG FPs.
