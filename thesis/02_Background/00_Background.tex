\chapter{Physical and modelling background}
\label{chap:physical-modelling-background}

The aim of this chapter is to provide a background to the topics of aerodynamic heating in hypersonic flow, turbulence modelling and wall functions. Each one of these topics can fill whole books, thus the following sections are not a holistic review of the topics, but rather an overview of the most relevant aspects to this thesis work. The reader is referred to the cited literature for more details.
In specific, the following sections will provide a background to the following topics:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Aerodynamic heating in hypersonic flow and the challenges of calculating the heat flux accuratly for conjugate heat transfer solvers.
    \item Compressible turbulent boundary layers, their important role in the flow physics and heat flux, and the challenges associated with them, especially over incompressible boundary layers. 
    \item RANS turbulence models and their limitations
    \item RANS wall-function fundamentals and the challenges of modelling near-wall flow
\end{itemize}
